# Ollie: complete product and design rebuild

## Outcome
A coherent parent-led setup and a calm, useful learning app for kids ages 4–12. This covers the **whole app**, not just onboarding: colors, type, buttons, artwork, motion, Search, Create, Daily Discovery, Trail, accounts and every parent screen. Preserve existing data and working interactions. No claims of guaranteed sales, safety, competitor superiority or App Store approval.

## 1. One visual system
- Keep **Geist**, royal blue **#4361EE** for decisive actions and sent messages, navy **#0E1538** for headings, and pale **#F2F4FC** surfaces. Add a restrained steel-blue atmospheric token for sky backgrounds, not a replacement action color. Parent screens use white surfaces and a steel-to-mist wash; kid screens remain light and neutral so topic art supplies character.
- Typography: onboarding headings 28/34, standard headings 24/30, body 16/24, supporting text 14/21, labels 12/16. Use navy titles, readable slate body and muted supporting text; no tiny low-contrast billing terms. Zero negative letter spacing.
- Cards 20px radius, input controls 16px, primary buttons pill-shaped and at least 52px tall; normal touch targets at least 48px, youngest mode 60px. One main filled action per screen. Secondary actions use clear text/icon or outline treatments. Shared Button, drawer and form components throughout.
- Preserve dashboard row colors: blue only Screen Time and Subscription; teal safety, pink reports, amber profile/Discovery settings. Gold only for actual rewards. No purple/coral UI accents.
- No nested cards, neon/glass gloss, decorative orbs or heavy shadows. Orb artwork remains an actual voice/thinking control. Clouds are bright, softly dimensional and slowly drifting, used only on open welcome/summary/handoff and Trail scenes, never dense forms, privacy, PIN, chat or prices.
- Layouts: safe-area-aware, dynamic viewport height, constrained content on wide screens, pinned actions with adequate content clearance. Verify 390×600, 390×844, 768×1024 and 1280×1800.

## 2. Artwork and mascot
- One original **tactile matte editorial** direction: softly sculpted real-world subjects, clean daylight, crisp silhouettes, restrained texture and readable subject relationships. No Ghibli imitation, glossy toddler worlds, oversized baby eyes or generic floating category icons.
- Retain the recognizable existing Ollie character and reconcile its proportions/materials across resting, listening, thinking, explaining and a small completion expression. Do not spend on a speculative mascot replacement.
- Create a shared reusable category-cover set and topic-specific scenes. A turtle lesson shows turtles; moon phases show phase relationships, not a generic planet scene. Covers, interests, Discovery introduction, actual slides and completed Trail nodes resolve through the same artwork registry.
- Preserve lightweight authored SVG where it demonstrates the topic accurately, but replace the current generic single-symbol scenes with layered, topic-specific editorial scenes. Generated bitmap covers complement these scenes, not substitute for accurate diagram/quiz content. Quizzes and recap use the relevant fact's scene.
- One art style across ages; younger layouts use simpler composition and larger subjects, older layouts richer diagrams and smaller mascot presence. No separate costly per-user art generation.

- Progress is a calm continuous track that reflects real remaining steps, with discreet phase dividers and no awkward chapter names. Back preserves choices; changing household count never silently deletes saved kid data.
- Use “kids,” not “children,” in product copy; legal wording may remain formal. All personalized copy must use current profile names.
- Motion: 220–280ms page transitions, 150ms tap feedback, small selection lift, one-time summary assembly and real completion feedback. No ubiquitous typewriter waits, fake loading delays, looping billing emphasis or guilt-based rewards. Existing paywall clip can play once briefly with a static reduced-motion poster, clear of terms and prices.

## 4. Kid app
**Search:** Keep the iMessage-like transcript and existing transport, centered Ollie identity and separate bottom tab dock. Transcript owns available height. Composer never overlaps tabs or keyboard. Under eight: clear large hold-to-speak orb in empty state with typing alternative, compact beside composer after conversation begins. Older kids: typing primary, compact voice. Release/cancel stops capture; visible listening/error states, no phone-call screen. Concise age/reading-matched answers, optional read-aloud, no engagement-bait follow-ups.

**Create:** A useful studio, not a cramped chip wall. Header with cookie balance; picture-builder/type modes; younger kids get visual Who/Doing/Where choices, older kids get a prompt composer and hold voice. One `Make my picture` action. Spacious square result, truthful generating/refund/error states, clean saved picture gallery and accessible full-view drawer. Keep 10 daily picture cookies, not 20. Remove fake paid-cookie credits: extra jars cannot grant anything without a verified purchase. No low-balance sales interruption after three pictures; finite allowance stated honestly.

**Daily Discovery:** Four discoveries + four matching questions + four recap/wonder slides; early reading band uses eight. Accurate shared age/reading-matched content and topic-specific art. Visible progress, consistent read-aloud control, safe quiz navigation, retry/hint states and clear finish. Complete today's Discovery before existing child tabs as specified. Replay never consumes the next lesson or repeats rewards.

**Trail:** An airy illustrated irregular path of actual completed-topic keepsakes. Missed days pause, never reset. Keep seven-learning-day jar and three-cookie bonus, plus existing one-cookie completion reward. Future stops are quiet, not urgency-pulsing. Completed scenes reopen exact saved content; no misleading category-only cover when the specific topic is available.

**Two kids:** Clear active-kid identity/switcher, separate chats, pictures, custom lessons, daily selection, completion, limits and reports. No accidentally shared local keys or stale voice playback on switching.

## 5. Parent Dashboard and account screens
- Preferred-name greeting, compact household kid switcher, no mixed activity feed by default.
- Hierarchy: selected kid's overview → learning/report and saved safety context → boundaries and Discovery controls → household account/subscription/privacy/device settings.
- Reports describe observed activity, not diagnoses, invented scores or fabricated achievements. Parent-only streak remains informational without pressure copy.
- Keep all current functional settings, PIN lockout, recovery, remote logout and deletion. Restyle every nested sheet, empty/error/loading state and account page consistently. Deletion covers the account and associated data; explain that deleting an account does not itself cancel Apple billing.
- Biometric unlock is device-local dashboard protection with PIN fallback, not account identity. Do not expose internal provider credentials or data in public MCP tools.

## 6. Reliability and Apple boundaries
- Populate and verify the currently empty shared lesson catalog/cache with repeatable migrations and real seed content, not pretend success. Daily selection must run once per kid/day, custom first-day lesson without bag advancement, shuffled 12-category rotation and per-category wraparound.
- Server-enforced access and quota checks before paid AI generation; client cookie counters are not security. Validate account ownership on every child resource. Keep actual model/provider availability and data routing truthful.
- Native digital memberships require StoreKit purchasing unless a documented storefront exception applies. Show subscription name, full localized price, period, trial eligibility/end, renewal and cancellation terms; meaningful restore/manage flows. Existing $99.99 yearly / $14.99 monthly defaults stay, with $8.33/month secondary annual breakdown. Prefer factual `Best value` over unsupported `Most Popular`; remove the inaccurate flat 40% badge or derive the actual discount.
- Trial reminder opt-in follows **successful trial start**, not a button that merely chooses a plan. Day-5 reminder includes selected kid reports, renewal date/price and cancellation route only after delivery is configured. Keep permission optional; never pretend browser permission means delivery.
- Apple/Google sign-in must actually be configured. Account deletion must be available in-app. Obtain explicit permission before sharing personal data with third-party AI, including processing begun in onboarding.
- General Education remains intended positioning; do not guarantee 4+ or category acceptance. Submit accurate age/audience and privacy questionnaires based on implemented functionality. Being outside Kids Category does not remove child privacy obligations.
- Sources: [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Subscriptions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/), [Account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion/), [Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility).
